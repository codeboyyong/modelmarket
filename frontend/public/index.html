About

Company

Model Market is a demo AI model marketplace for browsing multimodal models, comparing pricing, and testing workflows from a single workbench.

Mission

Make it simple for teams to discover image, audio, and video models without rebuilding provider integrations for every project.

Platform

The product combines a model catalog, credit wallet, routing gateway, and project workspace for generated artifacts.
